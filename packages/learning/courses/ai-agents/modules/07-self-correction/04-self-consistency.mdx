The simplest way to get independence is not to be cleverer. It is to ask the same question more
than once and see whether you get the same answer.

```
answers = [model(question) for _ in range(5)]
final   = most_common(answers)
```

That is the whole pattern. Its value comes from a property of sampling you built by hand in
[sampling](lesson:ai-llm-fundamentals.sampling): at any temperature above zero, a run is a
sample from a distribution rather than a lookup.

## Why the mode beats one draw

When a question has a clear answer, the distribution is concentrated on it, and five draws
mostly agree. When it does not, the draws scatter.

So the mode of five samples is more often right than the first sample, and — the part that
matters more — **the spread tells you which situation you were in**.

One sample gives you an answer and no idea how confident to be. Five samples give you an answer
and a distribution. Five identical answers and five different ones are very different states
that look identical if you only ever draw once.

## Temperature is a parameter of the method

At temperature 0 every sample is the same and you have paid five times for one answer. Set it
too high and the answers scatter for reasons that have nothing to do with difficulty, and the
mode is noise.

This is the one place where turning temperature *up* is the correct move, and it is worth
understanding why: you are deliberately buying variance in order to measure it. Moderate is the
right setting, and moderate has to be calibrated for the task rather than copied from a blog
post.

## Normalise before you count

The mode is only meaningful over answers that can be equal, and naive string comparison makes
almost nothing equal.

`"42"`, `"42."`, `"The answer is 42"` and `" 42 "` are one answer four ways, and counted raw you
get four groups of one and no majority. Normalise — trim, case-fold, strip punctuation, pull out
the number when the answer is numeric — before counting.

That normaliser is doing real work and it is also where the method quietly breaks. Too loose and
genuinely different answers collapse into agreement you did not earn. This is the same tension
as the detector in
[injection through a tool result](lesson:ai-agent-security.injection-through-a-tool-result):
you are choosing a threshold, and the honest version of the method reports what you chose.

## What to do with disagreement

Disagreement is the output people throw away, and it is the more useful half.

**High agreement** is a claim you can act on without further checking.

**A split** — three to two — is the case where the extra passes were worth buying, and it is a
signal to escalate: verify the claim, ask a human, or say the answer is uncertain rather than
picking the winner and reporting it flat.

**Total scatter** — five different answers — usually means the question is underspecified or the
information is not in the context. More sampling will not fix either.

Reporting a three-two split as a confident answer throws away the one thing the method
produced, and it is the most common way this pattern gets misused.

## When it does not apply

Self-consistency wants an answer that can be compared. A number, a category, a yes or no, a
short span of text.

It does not work on long generated prose, because two summaries are never equal and a
normaliser that makes them equal is measuring nothing. For prose, verify the claims inside it
with the previous lesson instead.

It also multiplies cost by n with no partial credit, so it belongs on the small number of
decisions that matter — a classification that routes a ticket, a number that goes in a report —
and not on everything.

## What the exercise asks

Sample n answers, normalise them, take the mode, and report the distribution alongside it.
Then a confidence band from the spread, and a rule that escalates a split rather than reporting
the winner as though it were unanimous.

The test that matters is the one where the answers come back 3-2 and the assertion is that the
result is marked uncertain. Getting the mode right is easy; refusing to sound confident about
it is the part with value.
