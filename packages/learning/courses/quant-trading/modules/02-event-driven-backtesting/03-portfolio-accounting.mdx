A trading strategy can hold simultaneous long and short positions across multiple assets while cash fluctuates from executions, commissions, and borrow fees.

A **portfolio ledger** tracks these positions deterministically.

## Position state and average cost basis

For each traded symbol, the ledger tracks:
* **Quantity**: Positive for long positions, negative for short positions, zero when flat.
* **Average entry price**: The volume-weighted execution price of currently open inventory.
* **Realized PnL**: Cumulative profit or loss locked in upon closing or reducing a position.

When adding to an existing position in the same direction (e.g. buying when already long):

$$
P_{\text{new\_avg}} = \frac{P_{\text{old\_avg}} \cdot Q_{\text{old}} + P_{\text{fill}} \cdot Q_{\text{fill}}}{Q_{\text{old}} + Q_{\text{fill}}}
$$

When reducing or closing a position (e.g. selling when long):
1. The average entry price remains unchanged.
2. The closed portion generates realized profit or loss:

$$
\Delta \text{PnL}_{\text{realized}} = (P_{\text{fill}} - P_{\text{avg}}) \cdot Q_{\text{closed}}
$$

For short positions, closing occurs upon buying, so $\Delta \text{PnL}_{\text{realized}} = (P_{\text{avg}} - P_{\text{fill}}) \cdot Q_{\text{closed}}$.

## Mark-to-market equity

At any timestamp, the total portfolio equity is the sum of settled cash and the market value of all open positions:

$$
\text{Total Equity} = \text{Cash} + \sum_{i} P_{\text{current}, i} \cdot Q_i
$$

For long positions ($Q_i > 0$), market value is positive. For short positions ($Q_i < 0$), market value acts as a liability reducing total equity until the position is bought back to cover.

## Exercise: build a portfolio ledger

In this exercise, you will implement a `PortfolioLedger` that:

1. Processes incoming `FillEvent` records, updating cash balances and deducting commissions.
2. Manages position quantities and updates average entry prices upon position expansion.
3. Calculates realized PnL upon position reduction or reversal.
4. Calculates total mark-to-market equity from current prices.
